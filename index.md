## About Me

I am a plant physiologist and molecular biologist specializing in **tree fruit physiology and molecular pomology**. My work focuses on understanding the physiological and molecular mechanisms regulating pre-harvest fruit drop, fruit ripening, abscission zone dynamics, and post-harvest storage quality in tree fruits, particularly apples and stone fruits.

I integrate physiological assays (internal ethylene concentration, fruit firmness, skin color metrics), biochemical evaluations (reactive oxygen species, oxidative stress markers), and high-throughput transcriptomics (RNA-Seq, DESeq2, qRT-PCR) to develop sustainable orchard management and storage strategies.
